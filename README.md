FRANTIC FLOWERS
A simple game for the Commodore 64.

Oh no! You forgot to get your girlfriend a gift, and she's already waiting in the park! Luckily there's plenty of flowers.
She loves red flowers. But she's allergic to white flowers, so if you pick one up, you'll have to drop any red flowers it touched!
Your girlfriend is in the park already. If she catches you before you're ready, it's all over.

Will you rise to the challenge and become the girl of her dreams? Or will you let her down for the last time?

Controls: WASD or arrow keys
